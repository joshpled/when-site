How it works

  1. Propose a few timesWhen reads your calendar so you only offer times you're actually open.
  2. Invite your friendsSend a link, or pick people from your contacts.
  3. Everyone votesNo group chat scheduling spiral. Just tap the times that work.
  4. Lock it inThe winning time goes on everyone's calendar, and When reminds you before it starts.
