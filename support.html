Calendars

Where do my plans get added?

By default, When creates its own calendar called When and adds plans there, so your plans stay separate from everything else and are easy to hide or delete as a group. You can change this in Settings → Calendars → Adds plans to, and choose your device's default calendar or any specific calendar you can write to.

I changed my calendar choice. Did my old plans move?

No. Plans already on your calendar stay where they are. Your choice applies to plans you add from that point on.

I deleted a plan from Apple Calendar. Can I add it back?

Yes. Open the plan in When and tap Add to Calendar again. When checks whether the event still exists before deciding what the button should do.

Does When see everything in my calendar?

When reads when you are busy so it can suggest times that work. It does not read the titles, invitees, or notes of your events.

Permissions

What if I say no?

When is built to keep working with any permission turned off, just with fewer features. Without calendar access it can't suggest times around your schedule or add plans to your calendar. Without contacts access you can still invite people with a share link. Without notifications you won't get reminders.

How do I change a permission later?

iOS Settings → When. Toggle contacts, calendars, or notifications. Changing a permission there closes the app, which is normal — reopen it and your data will be as you left it.

Invites

How do I invite someone?

From the Add Friend screen you can pick someone from your contacts, or share an invite link with anyone.

My friend tapped my link and nothing happened

If they don't have When installed, the link opens this website instead. Once they install the app, tapping the link again will open it directly.

Something is broken

Email hello@when.social and include what you were doing when it happened, and the version shown at the bottom of the Settings screen. That version string tells us exactly which build you're on, which usually cuts the guesswork in half.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To request deletion of anything stored on our backend, email us and we'll take care of it. See the privacy policy for the full picture.
