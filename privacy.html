When · Plans with Friends

Privacy Policy

Effective 5 August 2026 · Last updated 5 August 2026

When helps you and your friends find a time everyone is free. This policy explains what the app accesses on your device, what leaves your device, and what we never collect.

Questions about anything here? Email hello@when.social.

The short version

  • Your plans, friends list, and settings are stored on your device.
  • When reads your contacts and calendar only to make the app work. It does not upload your address book.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. There are no third-party trackers or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • Deleting the app deletes the data it stored.

What When accesses on your device

Contacts

If you allow contacts access, When reads your address book so you can pick people to invite without typing their details in. This happens on your device. Your contacts are not uploaded to a server, shared with anyone, or used for any purpose besides showing you a list to choose from.

You can decline contacts access and still use When — you can share an invite link instead. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings.

Calendar

If you allow calendar access, When does two things:

  • Reads your availability. When looks at when you are busy across your calendars so it can suggest times that work. It reads the times you are busy, not the contents, invitees, or notes of your events.
  • Writes plans you choose to add. When only creates a calendar event when you tap to add one. You choose which calendar it writes to, including a dedicated "When" calendar the app can create for you.

Calendar data stays on your device. You can decline calendar access and still use the rest of the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, When reminds you about upcoming plans and lets you know when something changes. Notification permission can be turned off at any time in iOS Settings.

What When stores

Plans, your friends list, your preferences, and your calendar destination choice are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. This data is included in your device backup if you back up your iPhone, and is governed by Apple's terms for that backup.

Accounts and syncing. When plans are shared between people, the information needed to deliver that plan — such as the plan's title, proposed times, and who was invited — is stored on our backend so it can reach the people you invited. We store the minimum needed to make shared plans work.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not show ads.
  • We do not upload your address book.
  • We do not read the contents of your calendar events.
  • We do not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Service providers

We use Supabase to host the backend that makes shared plans work. Supabase processes data on our behalf under its own terms. Apple provides TestFlight and the App Store, and may collect its own information about installs and crashes under Apple's privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Permissions. Contacts, calendar, and notification access can each be granted or revoked at any time in iOS Settings → When. The app is designed to keep working, with reduced features, when any of them is off.
  • Deleting your data. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To request deletion of anything stored on our backend, email hello@when.social and we will delete it.

Children

When is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Continuing to use When after a change means you accept the updated policy.
